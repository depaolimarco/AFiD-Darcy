# AFiD-Darcy documentation

AFiD, which stands for **A**dvanced **Fi**nite **D**ifference, is a highly parallelized finite difference Navier stokes solver developed jointly by University of Twente, SURFsara, and the University of Rome "Tor Vergata" to simulate primarily thermal convection in Rayleigh-Bénard flow. For the original publication, refer to [van der Poel et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compfluid.2015.04.007).

The current modified version of AFiD has been developed to numerically simulate flow through porous media, as governed by the Darcy equations [(De Paoli et al., 2025)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4995113).
